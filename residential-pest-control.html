Questions before you request?

Does Pestora provide the service itself?

No. We are an independent provider matching platform. Any service discussions, availability, scope, and pricing are determined by providers.

Can I request help for more than one concern?

Yes. Include the primary issue and additional context in your message so providers can assess the request.

Will I see a fixed price?

No. Providers set their own pricing and terms after reviewing the relevant information.
