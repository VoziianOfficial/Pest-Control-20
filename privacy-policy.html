Privacy Policy

How we handle information shared through Pestora.

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Overview

Pestora is an independent pest control connection platform. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you use our website or send a provider-matching request.

Information we collect

When you submit a request, we may collect your name, email address, service or pest issue, property type, and message. We also collect limited technical information needed to operate and secure the site.

How we use information

We use submitted information to review your request, respond by email, and facilitate potential connections with independent local providers. We do not sell personal information.

Independent providers

If we connect you with an independent provider, that business handles your information according to its own privacy practices. Provider availability, pricing, and terms are determined independently.
