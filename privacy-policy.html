Overview

Pestora is an independent pest control connection platform, not a pest control company. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website or submit a provider-matching request, why we collect it, how it is used and shared, and the choices available to you. By using our website or submitting a request, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

When you submit a request, we collect the details you choose to share so we can review it and pass it along to relevant providers. We also collect limited technical information needed to keep the site secure and working correctly.

  • Contact details you enter in a request form, such as name and email
  • Property and service information relevant to your pest concern
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device type, and approximate location from your IP address
  • Communications you send us directly by email

How we use information

We use submitted information to review your request, respond by email, and facilitate potential connections with independent local providers. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying information to understand how the site is used and to improve it.

  • Reviewing and responding to your request
  • Connecting you with relevant independent local providers
  • Maintaining the security and performance of our website
  • Complying with legal obligations where applicable

We do not sell personal information. We don't use your details for unrelated advertising or pass them to data brokers.

How we share information

We share information only where it's necessary to operate Pestora.

  • With independent providers you're matched with, so they can respond to your request
  • With service providers who help us run the website, such as hosting and email delivery, under confidentiality obligations
  • When required by law, such as in response to a valid legal request

We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Independent providers

If we connect you with an independent provider, that business handles your information according to its own privacy practices, which we encourage you to review. Provider availability, pricing, scope of service, and terms are determined independently by that provider, not by Pestora.

Ask providers directly. Because providers are independent businesses, we recommend asking any provider how they handle the information you share with them.

Retention & security

We retain request information for as long as needed to review your request, facilitate a provider connection, and meet our own recordkeeping and legal needs, after which it is deleted or anonymized. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from loss, misuse, or unauthorized access, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over the personal information we hold about you.

  • Access the information we have collected about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate details
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we're not required to keep it
  • Opt out of non-essential communications at any time

To exercise any of these choices, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

Pestora is intended for use by adults arranging pest control services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the site after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
