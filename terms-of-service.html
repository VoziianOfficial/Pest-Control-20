Matching process

When you submit a request, we may review the service category, general location, property type, timing, and notes you provide to decide whether the request appears suitable for provider routing. We may send your information to one or more independent providers that appear relevant, or we may decline to route a request if it is incomplete, outside scope, duplicative, abusive, fraudulent, unsafe, or unrelated to pest control.

  1. You submit contact and service details through the website.
  2. We review the request for basic fit, completeness, and routing relevance.
  3. An independent provider may contact you directly to discuss inspection, pricing, timing, and service terms.

Response times vary. Provider availability depends on local coverage, workload, service type, and whether the provider decides the request fits its business.
