A short explanation of essential website cookies.

Last updated: August 20, 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files saved by your browser that help websites remember limited information, such as whether you have made a cookie preference choice.

How Pestora uses cookies

We use essential cookies or local storage needed to remember your cookie preference and support basic website operation. We do not use cookies to provide pest control services or to set provider terms.

Your choices

When shown the cookie notice, you may accept or choose essential-only cookies. You can also change browser settings to manage stored data.
