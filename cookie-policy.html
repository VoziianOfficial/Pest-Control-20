What cookies are

Cookies are small files saved by your browser that help websites remember limited information, such as whether you have made a cookie preference choice. Similar technologies, like local storage, let a site remember information between visits without necessarily storing a traditional cookie file. This policy covers both.

How we use cookies

Pestora keeps things simple. We use your browser's local storage to remember one thing: whether you've responded to our cookie notice, and which choice you made. We don't use this to provide pest control services or to set any provider's terms.

  • Remembering your cookie preference so we don't ask again
  • Supporting basic website operation, like navigation and forms

Third-party content

Some pages load fonts, icons, or interactive elements from third-party services, such as Google Fonts for our typefaces. These requests may be visible to those providers as part of normal web delivery, separate from any cookie we set ourselves. We choose providers that are widely used and limit what we embed to what's needed for the page to work.

Managing your preferences

When the cookie notice appears, you can accept all or choose essential-only. You can also clear stored data at any time through your browser settings, which will reset your preference and show the notice again on your next visit.

Changes to this policy

If the way we use cookies or similar technology changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
