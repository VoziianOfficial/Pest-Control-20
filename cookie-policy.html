What cookies are

Cookies are small files saved by your browser that help websites remember limited information, such as whether you have made a cookie preference choice. Similar technologies, like local storage, let a site remember information between visits without necessarily storing a traditional cookie file. This policy covers both.

In everyday terms, cookies and local storage help a website avoid asking the same basic question every time a page loads. They can also support security, forms, preferences, language settings, analytics, advertising, or embedded content depending on how a site is built. currently uses a narrow version of this technology for preference storage and basic website operation.

How we use cookies

Third-party content

Some pages load fonts, icons, or interactive elements from third-party services, such as Google Fonts for our typefaces. These requests may be visible to those providers as part of normal web delivery, separate from any cookie we set ourselves. We choose providers that are widely used and limit what we embed to what's needed for the page to work.

Third-party services may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and the page requesting the asset. This is part of standard web delivery. We do not control every technical choice made by those providers, so their own policies may explain additional practices.

Managing your preferences

When the cookie notice appears, you can accept all or choose essential-only. You can also clear stored data at any time through your browser settings, which will reset your preference and show the notice again on your next visit.

  1. Open your browser settings or privacy panel.
  2. Find cookies, site data, storage, or permissions for this website.
  3. Clear the stored preference if you want the notice to appear again.

Browser controls

Most browsers allow you to block cookies, delete stored data, set site-specific permissions, or use private browsing modes. These controls are managed by your browser, not by . Blocking all storage may make some website features less convenient, because the site may be unable to remember that you already responded to the cookie notice.

If you use multiple browsers or devices, your choices may need to be set separately on each one. Clearing all browser data can also remove unrelated saved sessions, preferences, or passwords, so review your browser's prompts before confirming a deletion.
